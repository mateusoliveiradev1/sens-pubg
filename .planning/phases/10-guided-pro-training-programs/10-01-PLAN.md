- HIGH: Ciclo Pro becomes a static course or generic habit tracker. Mitigation: every mission must include evidence-bound anatomy and mutate program state.
- HIGH: calendar completion fakes progress. Mitigation: week closure and difficulty/aggressiveness advancement are separate; evidence convergence gates advancement.

</threat_model>
