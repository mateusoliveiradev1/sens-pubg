<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| client invite/workspace input -> DB | Workspace names, role requests, invite tokens, and seat counts are untrusted. |
| public community squad -> private Team workspace | Community membership must not grant private coach access. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-13-02-01 | Elevation of Privilege | workspace actions | mitigate | Reload access/membership/role server-side before every write. |
| T-13-02-02 | Tampering | seat counts | mitigate | Compute seat summary from persisted membership/invite state. |
| T-13-02-03 | Repudiation | invite/member lifecycle | mitigate | Persist audit events for invite, role, membership, seat, and archive events. |
| T-13-02-04 | Information Disclosure | workspace identity | mitigate | Keep Team workspaces private and separate from public squad identity. |
</threat_model>
